I'm a third-year Computer Science student with a minor in Robotics at Northeastern University. My research spans Human-Robot Interaction and Animal-Computer Interaction, with work published at HRI '26 and CHI '26.

I work in the PARCS Lab (Prof. Zhi Tan) studying how robots interact with people, and the INTERACT Animal Lab (Prof. Rebecca Kleinberger) designing technology for animals.
