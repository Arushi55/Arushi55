### Hi, I'm Arushi!

I'm a third-year Computer Science student at Northeastern University with a concentration in Human-Centered Computing. My research sits at the intersection of HCI, robotics, and the natural world — I study how people interact with robots, and how we can build technology that genuinely serves animals too.

---

**Research**

- **Human-Robot Interaction** — PARCS Lab (Prof. Zhi Tan)
  How a robot's physical and social presentation shapes people's willingness to engage with it. Published at [HRI '26](https://doi.org/10.1145/3757279.3785555):
  *"Meet My Sidekick!": Effects of Separate Identities and Control of a Single Robot in HRI*

- **Animal-Computer Interaction** — INTERACT Animal Lab (Prof. Rebecca Kleinberger)
  Designing technology systems built *for* animals — centering animal agency and experience rather than just human convenience. Published at [CHI '26](https://doi.org/10.1145/3772318.3790842):
  *BearBubbles: Interactive Olfactory Enrichment to Encourage Foraging in Zoo Animals*
